## Selected Research

### World Action Model / RVT-2 Future Ranker
Ongoing research on future-state reasoning for robotic manipulation. I am exploring how learned future representations and expert-alignment scoring can rerank RVT-2 action candidates in RLBench, with the goal of improving action selection beyond a purely reactive policy.

**Topics:** RVT-2 · RLBench · Transformers · Action Ranking · Future Representation

---

### PrecisionReach-GS
A real-to-sim robot learning pipeline that combines calibrated wrist-camera perception, 3D Gaussian Splatting, and vision-language-action policies for precise robotic reaching and manipulation.

**Status:** Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Robot Learning  
**Topics:** Robot Learning · VLA · 3DGS · Real-to-Sim · Robot Calibration

---

### GUARD — Geometric Uncertainty-Aware Robust Denoiser
A point-cloud framework that combines geometric representation learning with random Fourier feature Gaussian-process uncertainty for robust denoising and segmentation in a single forward pass.

**Status:** Submitted to Advanced Engineering Informatics  
**Code:** [GUARD_Point_denoiser](https://github.com/001-Wang/GUARD_Point_denoiser)  
**Project Page:** [001-wang.github.io/GUARD_Point_denoiser](https://001-wang.github.io/GUARD_Point_denoiser/)

---

### Semantic Gaussian Splatting for 3D Damage Modeling
A scalable multi-view framework that learns persistent per-Gaussian semantic and damage attributes from sparse 2D supervision for consistent 3D localization across views.

**Code & Demo:** [Probability-Gaussians](https://github.com/001-Wang/Probability-Gaussians)  
**Topics:** 3DGS · Multi-View Geometry · 3D Segmentation · CUDA
